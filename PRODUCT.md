# Product

## Register

product

## Users

D&D players and Dungeon Masters who manage their tabletop RPG experience inside Obsidian. Players track character state (HP, spell slots, abilities, inventory) during and between sessions. DMs run live encounters (initiative, monster stats, session logs) and build compendiums of rules and spells. Both groups value information density and quick access over flashy interaction.

## Product Purpose

An Obsidian plugin that transforms markdown into interactive, themed RPG UI components. It bridges the gap between the flexibility of a personal knowledge base and the structured needs of tabletop play. Success means players never leave Obsidian to manage their character, and DMs can run an entire session from their vault.

## Brand Personality

Arcane craftsman. Precise, deliberate, with subtle fantasy warmth. Every element feels intentionally placed, like a well-made artifact rather than mass-produced software. The tone is confident and understated, not whimsical or loud.

Three words: **precise, deliberate, crafted.**

## Anti-references

- **SaaS generic:** White/blue blandness, round cards, hero metrics, corporate neutrality. This is not a dashboard.
- **Gamer overload:** Neon colors, aggressive styling, competing panels, visual noise. Density is not clutter.
- **Unstyled / skeletal:** Browser defaults with minor tweaks. This plugin earns its visual presence.

## Design Principles

1. **Density with clarity.** Show a lot of information without feeling cramped. Hierarchy and spacing do the work, not separators and boxes.
2. **Themed but functional.** Fantasy warmth should reinforce comprehension, not distract from it. Every decorative choice must also serve a structural purpose.
3. **Plugin-native.** Respect Obsidian's conventions (theming, layout, keyboard). The plugin should feel like a natural extension, not an embedded foreign app.
4. **Quiet confidence.** Let the content (stats, spells, rules) be the star. The UI recedes when you're reading and surfaces when you need to interact.
5. **Crafted details.** Small touches (typographic choices, subtle color shifts, considered spacing) signal intentionality. Nothing is accidental.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance for contrast ratios
- Full respect for Obsidian's dark/light theme system
- Keyboard navigation for interactive elements (initiative controls, HP adjustments, spell slot toggles)
- Reduced motion: honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for any transitions
