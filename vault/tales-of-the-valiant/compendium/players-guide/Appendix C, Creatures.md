*Complete transcription of the "**Tales of the Valiant** Player's Guide" book by **Kobold Press**.*

This appendix contains statistics for beasts and vermin that serve as familiars or mounts for PCs. It also contains creatures suitable for druids using the Beast Form feature or creatures that might appear when a PC casts a spell, such as _animate dead_.
