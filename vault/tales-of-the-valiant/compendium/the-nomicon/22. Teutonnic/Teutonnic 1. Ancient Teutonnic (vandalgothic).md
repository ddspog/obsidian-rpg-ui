# Ancient Teutonnic (vandalgothic)
This naming group is based on the Germanic tribes that swept into the Roman Empire.
## Tribal names, Cultures, Countries
Countries and cultures are generated from the base tribal name. Endings can be -land (a very old word), -heim, or just -ia. So, Aegoths might come from Aegothland, Aegotland, Aegutland, or Aegothia. The Aolterni might come from Aolterland, Aolterheim, or Aolternia, depending on where you divide the word into a tribal name and an ending.
