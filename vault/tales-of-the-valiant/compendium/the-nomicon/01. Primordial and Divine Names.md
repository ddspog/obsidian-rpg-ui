# Naming Other Deities
Other than the cosmic eldritch horror deities, naming deities is a wide-open and highly subjective process. The names of the deities ought to sound similar in tone, but it's also important to make the names different enough for the players to remember them.

Probably the best table for short, unfamiliar names that have gravitas to them is actually the Continent Names table. It's designed for continent names, but these short and relatively powerful names can also work well for generating deity names such as Eülchar, Guastis, and Jhomnon.

The Colossoponderous Single-Word Place or Deity Names table is another workable table for deity names, generating results such as Azugulma, Djangahloon, Rehokanthu, and Tashamaja.

Nanskrit is a good naming group for general deities. The Nanskrit Masculine Personal Names table generates names such as Chadayesh, Harakaj, Jayamal, and Pridhatri. The Nanskrit Personal Feminine Names table generates goddess names such as Jika, Neelula, and Hadhana. All of these have the gravitas associated with deity names.

For more Celtic-sounding deities, the Brittonian Personal Names tables work well, just as Norse-sounding deities should use the Nörslik Tables and Greek-type deities should use Hellenica.
