![[brittonnian.webp|right|168]]Brittonnian mimics a group of languages called “Insular Celtic,” which is the language family of the Celts of the British Isles. This family contained two language families, the Goidelic Celts (Irish, Manx, Scottish Gaelic) and the Brythonic Celts (Welsh, Breton, Cornish).

Remember that a “w” makes an “oo” sound as a whole syllable.
