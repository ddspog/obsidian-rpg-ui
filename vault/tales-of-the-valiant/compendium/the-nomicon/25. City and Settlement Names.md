You’ll be able to name most of your cities and settlements using the City/Settlement or the Terrain Feature tables in any of the naming groups. There is one other method that can be used in any of the naming groups, which is the use of an item or short, mundane word for the city name. A good example of this is in Glen Cook’s Black Company series, where many of the cities have names like “Roses” or “Opal.”

This table generates city names such as “Earrings,” “Bags,” and “Silk.”

**Instructions**: Roll on any of the four columns for a city name that stands alone. It’s not “Welcome to the City of Alembic,” it’s just “Welcome to Alembic.”
**Sample Results**: Alembic, Salamander, Fortune, Tongs.

# Street Names
## List of Street Types/names
When you’re naming streets, use as much variation as possible, or the map will look repetitious.
### The Full List:
Alley, Avenue, Boulevard, Circle, Close (a dead end or a narrow passage between buildings), Gate, Lane, Market, Mews (lane), Road, Square, Street, Trace, Walk, Way. Not all of these are used in the tables below because many of them are specific types of streets.
