## Cities and Settlements
These tables can be used for countries, but they are designed primarily for cities and settlements. The two tables are similar, but they are divided into two tables in order to avoid certain unpronounceable or difficult combinations of consonants (as an example, “Aprararos” would be a possible outcome if the tables weren’t divided).
