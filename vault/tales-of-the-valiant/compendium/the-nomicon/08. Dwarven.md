# Pure Dwarven (khuzdulak)
This naming group is based on the general sound of Tolkien’s dwarven language, which he described as a secret language; most dwarves learned it as children, but their cradle-languages were those of the humans they lived near.
- **Method 1**. Roll a Part 1 and Part 2 on [[08. Dwarven#Table 8-1 Dwarven Place Names Table 1|Table 8-1]]. Each result is a separate word, such as Khab Khedar.
- **Method 2**. Roll a Part 1 and Part 2, but switch the order, so there is a two-syllable starting word and a one-syllable ending word, such as Khiruk Khal or Azak Zul.
- **Method 3**. Roll a Part 1 result on column 1 and on column 2, for two words (the order doesn’t matter) with one syllable each, such as Khab Khes, Khes Khab, Khal Thrain, or Thrain Khal.
