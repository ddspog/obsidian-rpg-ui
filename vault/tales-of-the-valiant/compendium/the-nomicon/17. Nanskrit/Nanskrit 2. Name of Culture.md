# Name of Culture
To get a name for the culture, roll or choose a result on [[Nanskrit 2. Name of Culture#Table 17-3 Nanskrit Culture Names|Table 17-3, Name of the Culture]], rolling on Column 1 for a first part, and then on one of the other columns for an ending. The assumption is that your first culture in this naming group will use Dialect 1, but that if you get far enough away you might choose to switch to Dialect 2. Or you can just switch them up for additional variation in the names.

If it ends in an “i,” (e.g., Amajathi), that’s the name and adjectival form of the culture’s name. The Amajathi Empire would rule the Amajathi land and the Amajathi people and culture.

If it ends in a vowel other than “i,” (e.g., Amaghota), the adjectival form ends with -shi, or -man. Amaghota would be the Empire (or land) of Amaghota, but the Amaghotaman (or Amaghoshi) Empire or Culture.

If it ends in a “t,” the adjectival form ends with an -i or -tti. For example, Amachut would be the name of the Empire and region (the Amachut Empire, the Amachut region), but would be referred to as the Amachuti or Amachutti Empire or culture.

If it ends in a consonant other than “t,” (e.g., Amaganar), the adjectival form ends with -ic or -an. Amaganar would be the name of the Empire or the region, but it would be referred to as the Amaganaric or Amaganaran Empire or culture.
