![[eurovesian.webp|right|284]]Eurovesian is a relatively generic naming grouping for when you want a generally European feel to the names, but don’t care about being more specific than that.

There aren’t any specific personal names tables for Eurovesian because it’s a jumble of all Europe. For personal names you can use any of the European type naming groups ([[04. Anglish|Anglish]], [[06. Brittonnian|Brittonian]], [[23. Trobadoric|Trobadoric]], [[15. Italican|Italican]], [[14. Hellenica|Hellenica]], etc.).
