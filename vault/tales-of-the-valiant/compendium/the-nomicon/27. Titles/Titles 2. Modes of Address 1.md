## Ordinary Monarchs
The format is a Title Adjective ([[27. Titles#Table 27-2 Title Adjective|Table 27-2]]) plus a Noun of Majesty ([[27. Titles#Table 27-8 Nouns of Majesty, Table 1|Tables 27-8]] and [[27. Titles#Table 27-9 Nouns of Majesty, Table 2|27-9]].
#### Table 27-2: Title Adjective
```rpg table.title-adjective
|dice:d100|Table for Adjective|
|---|---|
|01-20|Admired ([[27. Titles#Table 27-3 Admired|Table 27-3]]])|
|21-40|Capacity and Metaphors ([[27. Titles#Table 27-4 Capacity and Metaphors|Table 27-4]])|
|41-60|Holy or Reverent ([[27. Titles#Table 27-5 Holy or Reverent|Table 27-5]])|

|81-100|Powerful ([[27. Titles#Table 27-7 Powerful|Table 27-7]])|
```

## Tyrants
The format is a Tyrannical Title Adjective ([[27. Titles#Table 27-10 Tyrannical Title Adjective|Table 27-10]]) plus a Noun of Majesty. I recommend just sticking with Majesty, Highness, or Grace (the normal nouns for monarchs), but you can add weirdness to the title using a generated Noun of Majesty ([[27. Titles#Table 27-8 Nouns of Majesty, Table 1|Tables 27-8]] and [[27. Titles#Table 27-9 Nouns of Majesty, Table 2|27-9]]).
