# East-asiantic Cultures and Countries
This group of phonemes comes from place names across Southeast Asia and into East Asia. The names are written to be easily pronounced by an English speaker, which means they are inaccurate but useful for the purpose.

## East Asiantic Terrain features
For “the Forest of Bakuo,” or the “Huongdai Marshes.”

The sources for these phonemes stretch from just east of India all the way into China, so there is a lot of Indo-European in here. Also, Southeast Asian cultures had a lot of interaction with West Africa, so there are African sounds in here as well.
