![[metallik.webp|right|264]]Metallik has a powerful sound and rhythm, drawing mostly on Indo-European (Sanskrit) and Ancient Greek. Dharc Metallik uses phonemes that are more sinister and threatening, and High Metallik uses more heroic-sounding phonemes.

This naming group is really about places, not people. Any naming group will work for the normal, everyday people; it’s the powerful warriors and spellcasters who tend to have distinctive names in the fiction that drives these naming groups. Those can be named using [[17. Nanskrit|Nanskrit]], [[18. Nörslik|Nörslik]], or [[23. Trobadoric|Trobadoric]], or even Dreamlandish for powerful spellcasters (see [[Elderweirdish 3. Dreamlandish 1#Table 10-6 Dreamlandish Powerful Spellcasters Masculine Short Name|Table 10-6: Dreamlandish Powerful Spellcasters Masculine Short Name]] and the following tables). That said, the place names tables (Table 3 in particular) do work for generating personal names as well.
