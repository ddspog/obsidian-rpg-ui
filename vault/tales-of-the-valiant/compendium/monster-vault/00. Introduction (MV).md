# 00. Introduction
*Complete transcription of the "**Tales of the Valiant** Monster Vault" book by **Kobold Press**.*

Fantasy worlds are full of mysterious and magical creatures. Encounters with these monsters are a cornerstone of adventure in the **Tales of the Valiant™** roleplaying game. Whether outwitting them or locked in deadly struggles, the monsters in this book serve as the foes and foils to your cast of player characters (PCs). 

Every monster herein comes with a bounty of lore ready to inspire your next game. However, these entries are ultimately up to your interpretation. As the game master (GM), you arbitrate how these creatures inhabit your world and how they come into conflict with PCs in your game. Deciding where these monsters live, how they act, and what they want are key components in bringing them to life.
## What are Monsters?
Every being in a **Tales of the Valiant™** game (including each PC) is a creature, especially when referenced in rules text. In descriptive text, the terms “creature” and “monster” are mostly interchangeable, but “monster” is primarily used to describe any creature that can be fought and potentially killed. This book is most valuable as a tool for running combat encounters, and it leans into the dangerous natures of the denizens covered within it. Most monsters are obvious threats to peaceful creatures, spreading terror and chaos until stopped by a band of valiant heroes.
# Using this book
![[brass-dragon-wyrmling.webp|right|384]] Written primarily for GMs, this book features over 400 creatures to use in your games. For PCs, this book features a handful of familiars and beasts perfect for characters seeking companions or druids seeking animals and beasts for Wild Shape. If you are a player reading this book, check with your GM before selecting any of these creatures as familiars or companions for Wild Shape. 

To use this book, you will need the **Tales of the Valiant™** _Player’s Guide_ for the core rules of the **Tales of the Valiant™** roleplaying game. Spell, disease, and magic item names that appear in _italics_ without a page or book reference can be found in the _Player’s Guide_.
## What's in the book?
[[01. Using Monsters|Chapter 1 (Using Monsters)]] gives you guidance on building monster-centric encounters, including their roles and tactics. It provides optional rules for increasing the challenge of encounters (Doom) and strategies for altering existing monsters to better suit your needs (Modifying Monsters). 

[[02. Understanding Monster Statistics|Chapter 2 (Understanding Monster Statistics)]] describes the elements of the monster stat blocks in this book. 

[[03. Understanding Terrain and Creature Types|Chapter 3 (Understanding Terrain and Creature Types)]] includes a list of the common terrain types where monsters are encountered, and it details the common traits, themes, and allies of various creature types. 

[[04. A to Z, Monsters|Chapter 4 (Monsters A to Z)]] features the lore and statistics for hundreds of fantasy monsters, from the familiar aboleth to the new wyrdling. 

[[Appendix, Animals|Animals Appendix]] adds almost 100 common animals, from bats to rats to cats and many more! 

[[Appendix, NPCs|NPCs Appendix]] presents over two dozen stat blocks for non-player characters to fully flesh out your world, from acolytes and cultists to bards and knights. 

[[Appendix, Creatures by Challenge|Tables Appendix]] offers a dozen reference tables to find the exact monster you need for your game, listing monsters by challenge ratings, tags, and terrains.
