# 02. Understanding Monster Statistics
*Complete transcription of the "**Tales of the Valiant** Monster Vault" book by **Kobold Press**.*

Rules for understanding the statistics of monsters (often called “creatures”) are described in this chapter. If you’re a Game Master (GM), look this over and familiarize yourself with the elements of a monster and its statistics before using it in your game. 

**_Description and Lore._** Each monster entry includes an italicized description of it as well as lore describing its origins, typical motives, and similar information. The italicized description at the top of each entry is written as if the player characters (PCs) just stumbled upon the creature, and the description often can be read aloud to your players when their characters first encounter it. 

**_Behind-the-Scenes Calculations._** The stat blocks in this book include all of the saves, DCs, attack rolls, and similar numbers already calculated to give you everything you need to effectively use the creature during a game session. The calculations listed in this chapter give you a behind-the-scenes look at how some of those numbers were derived. 
# Elements of a Stat Block 
The information you need to use a creature in your game is organized into an easy-reference format called a statistics block or stat block. This section describes the meaning of the terms in a stat block and the contents of each heading within the stat block. Note that each creature’s stat block has been written and organized with an eye toward making creatures faster and easier to use during your game, including displaying the precalculated results for ability checks, damage, ability scores, and other calculations.
