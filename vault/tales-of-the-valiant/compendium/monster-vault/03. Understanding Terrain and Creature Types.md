# 03. Understanding Terrain and Creature Types
![[terrain-tools.webp|right|384]]*Complete transcription of the "**Tales of the Valiant** Monster Vault" book by **Kobold Press**.*

This chapter describes where creatures might be encountered, and it gives guidance on typical allies, traits, and encounter locations for each creature type. If you are looking for inspiration for an adventure site or for the kinds of allies your favorite monster might have, you’ll find it here. 
# Terrain 
Because most creatures can inhabit multiple types of terrain, the creatures in this book have been divided into the terrains they inhabit in the Creatures by Terrain appendix rather than duplicated in the various terrains listed here. See that appendix for the broadest lists showing which creatures would best fit an encounter and its terrain.

# Creature Types 
A creature’s type describes its fundamental nature. Certain spells, magic items, class features, and other effects interact in special ways with creatures of a particular type. For example, an [[arrow of dragon slaying]] deals extra damage to creatures of the [[Dragon]] type, such as red dragons, dragon turtles, and wyverns. This section includes definitions, allies, common traits and themes, and typical encounter locations for each of the creature types in this book. Each type section also includes a table of each creature of that type in this book along with its CR, its common allies, where you can find it in this book, and how to pronounce its name. 

**_Pronunciation Key._** Each creature includes guidance on how to pronounce its name. The guidance is written using phonetic spelling with no special characters for easier pronunciation.
