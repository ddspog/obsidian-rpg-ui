# 01. Using Monsters
*Complete transcription of the "**Tales of the Valiant** Monster Vault" book by **Kobold Press**.*

This chapter shows you how to craft monster-centric encounters your players will love, including how to calculate the challenge of combat scenarios, an overview of monster roles, and basic strategies for creature customization.
# Building Monster-Centric Encounters
Adventures are built from a series of encounters—each one like a scene in a movie or TV show. In some encounters, the PCs talk to NPCs. In others, the PCs explore a location. And in many encounters, the PCs fight monsters. This section focuses on the last of these: crossing swords with one’s foes and staring into the burning eyes of one’s enemy.
