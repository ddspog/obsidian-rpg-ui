```rpg feature.danger
name: Torrential Precipitation
type: Hazard
trigger: This hazard begins when a creature enters an area affected by torrential precipitation. 
effects: Everything within the area is considered lightly obscured (see [[Senses#Vision and Obscured Areas|Vision and Obscured Areas]]). A creature that starts its turn within torrential precipitation has [[disadvantage]] on WIS ([[Perception]]) checks. If the torrential precipitation is heavy rain, it extinguishes open flame. If it is heavy snowfall, the ground is difficult terrain. 
resolution: An affected creature must find shelter or leave the area.
---
Precipitation such as heavy rain or snowfall causes a variety of problems. It can last from minutes to days. 
```
