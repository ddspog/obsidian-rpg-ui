```rpg feature.danger
name: Extreme Heat
type: Hazard
trigger: This hazard begins when a creature is exposed to temperatures above 100 degrees Fahrenheit for at least 1 hour without access to drinkable water. A creature avoids exposure if it is resistant or immune to fire damage, or has natural adaptation to hot climates.
effects: After the first hour, an exposed creature must succeed on a DC 5 CON save or gain one level of exhaustion. The creature repeats this save at the end of each hour of continued exposure, increasing the DC by 1 for each hour beyond the first. Creatures wearing medium or heavy armor, or wrapped in heavy clothing, have disadvantage on the save.
resolution: If an exposed creature drinks ample water and is removed from the affected area for at least 1 hour, the CON save resets to DC 5. Otherwise, this hazard ends once a creature leaves the affected area or environmental conditions significantly change.
---
Exposure to temperatures above 100 degrees Fahrenheit threatens creatures without access to drinkable water. The hazard is generally continual.
```
