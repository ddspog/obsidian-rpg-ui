# Goodberry
````rpg spell
circle: 1st-Circle
source:
  - "[[Primordial]]"
school: Conjuration
casting: 1 action
range: Touch
components:
  - V
  - S
  - M (a handful of berries)
duration: Instantaneous
summary: Ten berries, heal 1 HP each.
image: "![[goodberry.webp|384]]"

---
You transform up to ten berries in your hand, infusing them with magic for up to 24 hours. A creature can use its action to eat one berry.

```rpg rule.content
id: item
---
Eating a berry restores 1 HP and provides enough nourishment to sustain a creature for one day, though a creature can’t be sustained by eating berries two or more days in a row.

The berries lose their potency if they have not been consumed within 24 hours of the casting of this spell.
```
````
