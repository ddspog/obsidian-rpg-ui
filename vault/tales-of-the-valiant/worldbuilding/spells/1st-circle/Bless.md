# Bless
```rpg spell
circle: 1st-Circle
source:
  - "[[Divine]]"
school: Enchantment
casting: 1 action
range: 30 feet
components:
  - V
  - S
  - M (a sprinkling of holy water)
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
summary: Allies add d4 to rolls.
image: "![[bless.webp|384]]"

---
You bless up to three creatures of your choice within range. When a target makes an attack roll or a save before the spell ends, the target can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the attack roll or save.<br/><br/>

**_At Higher Circles._** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd circle or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot above 1st.
```
