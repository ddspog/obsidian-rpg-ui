# Clue º
```rpg spell
circle: 1st-Circle Ritual
source:
  - "[[Arcane]]"
  - "[[Divine]]"
  - "[[Primordial]]"
school: Divination
casting: 1 minute
range: Touch
components:
  - V
  - S
  - M (a magnifying glass and pipe)
duration: 10 minutes
style:
  - Dream
summary: Glow nearby footprints and fingerprints.
image: "![[clue.webp|384]]"

---
When you cast this spell, all footprints and fingerprints within a 45-foot radius of a point you touch become highlighted and glow faintly for the duration. At the time of casting, choose any period of time up to the last 10 days to the present; only footprints and fingerprints left within that time will be highlighted. Each creature whose footprints or fingerprints are detected by the spell is assigned a unique color, but are not otherwise identified. Any creature that moves or touches objects in the area will also leave colorful footprints and fingerprints, which might reveal invisible creatures in the area. <br/><br/>

**_At Higher Circles._** As you unlock higher circles, this ritual grows in potency. Once you have access to 4th-circle spell slots, you can use this spell to access a period of time up to the last month to the present.
```
