# Component Pouch
```rpg item.element
name: Component Pouch
type: "[[Adventuring Gear]]"
cost: 25 gp
weight: 2 lb.
image: "![[component-pouch.webp|384]]"
shop:
  cheap: 18 gp
  expensive: 38 gp
  availability:
    - "[[Premium]]"
---
A component pouch is a small, watertight leather belt pouch that has compartments to hold all the material components and other special items you need to cast your spells (see [[07. Spellcasting|Components]]), except for those material components that have a listed gp cost (as indicated in a spell’s description).
```
