# Pole (10-foot)
```rpg item.element
name: Pole (10-foot)
type: "[[Adventuring Gear]]"
cost: 5 cp
weight: 7 lb.
image: "![[pole.webp|384]]"
shop:
  cheap: 3 cp
  expensive: 8 cp
  availability:
    - "[[Rural]]"
    - "[[Urbane]]"
    - "[[Premium]]"
---
A Pole is 10 feet long. You can use it to touch something up to 10 feet away. If you must make a Strength ([[Athletics]]) check as part of a [[High Jump]] or [[Long Jump]], you can use the Pole to vault, giving yourself [[Advantage]] on the check.
```
