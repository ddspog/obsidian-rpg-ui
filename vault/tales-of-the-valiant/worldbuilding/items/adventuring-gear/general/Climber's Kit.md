# Climber's Kit
```rpg item.element
name: Climber's Kit
type: "[[Adventuring Gear]]"
cost: 25 gp
weight: 12 lb.
image: "![[climbers-kit.webp|384]]"
shop:
  cheap: 18 gp
  expensive: 38 gp
  availability:
    - "[[Urbane]]"
    - "[[Premium]]"
---
A climber’s kit includes special pitons, boot tips, gloves, and a harness. You can use the climber’s kit as an action to anchor yourself to a solid surface within 5 feet of you (this surface can’t be part of a creature); when you do, you can’t fall more than 25 feet from your anchor point, and you can’t climb more than 25 feet away from that point without undoing the anchor.
```
