# Rations (1 day)
```rpg item.element
name: Rations (1 day)
type: "[[Adventuring Gear]] (Consumable)"

Rations consist of dry foods suitable for extended travel, including jerky, dried fruit, hardtack, and nuts. One unit of rations is enough to feed one Medium or smaller creature for one day.
```
