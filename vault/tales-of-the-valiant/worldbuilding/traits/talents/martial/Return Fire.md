# Return Fire
```rpg feature.details
reaction:
  text: |
    You are skilled at suppressing long-ranged foes in combat. When a creature you can see hits or misses you with a ranged attack, as a reaction you can make a single weapon attack against that creature with a [[ranged]] or [[thrown]] weapon if the target is within your weapon's range (short or long).
```
