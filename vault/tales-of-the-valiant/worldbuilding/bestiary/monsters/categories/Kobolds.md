>Three short, draconic humanoids scurry out of the shadows, reptilian tails whipping back-and-forth with excitement. One grins mischievously while holding a contraption, another twirls a dagger with eyes alert to danger, and the third lights a candle on its miner’s cap.

![[kobolds-category.webp|right|414x555]] Cowardly cousins to chromatic dragons, [[kobolds]] serve draconic overlords as warriors and servants. These scrappy menaces mimic the behaviors of their dragon masters. Though their small stature and recklessness make kobolds poor imitators of [[Dragon|dragons]], what they lack in ferocity they make up for in zeal and ingenuity. They are especially adept at creating traps and setting ambushes.
Kobolds’ scales resemble those of chromatic dragons that live near their warrens. Rarely, kobolds possess features evocative of metallic dragons or other dragon-like creatures.

**Practical Minions**. Kobolds are diligent, hardworking creatures who view life through a practical lens. They often work beside larger, more powerful creatures, especially dragons, because it is more practical to work with such creatures than defy them and get eaten. Whether the larger creatures are dragons or humans, the relationship is typically mutually beneficial for both, and many kobold clans serve specific dragons or human family lines for generations.

**Clever Miners**. Though kobolds can be found almost anywhere, they are most at home underground, in dark forests, or in the shadowed streets of close-built cities. Most are skilled miners and have at least a passing interest in alchemical substances or mechanical devices. Because of this, most kobold clan lairs are filled with traps.

**Scaly Teamwork**. Kobolds share a strong communal instinct, knowing that only by acting together can they hope to defeat or survive threats against them. Young are hatched and raised in clutches, and many kobolds spend their entire lives close to their clutchmates, even if each pursues a different field. Kobolds without a clan or traveling the world often extend this mentality to their traveling companions, viewing even non-kobolds as their clutchmates.

**Assassins and Alchemists**. Kobolds are quick studies and curious about anything and everything. This leads some kobolds to seek lives of excitement and new thrills as spies, assassins, and covert messengers, and it leads others to pursue lives of tinkering or witchcraft, where they create wondrous machines or strange potions.
