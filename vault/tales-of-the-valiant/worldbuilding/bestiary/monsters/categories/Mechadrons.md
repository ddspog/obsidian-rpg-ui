>Three short, draconic humanoids scurry out of the shadows, reptilian tails whipping back-and-forth with excitement. One grins mischievously while holding a contraption, another twirls a dagger with eyes alert to danger, and the third lights a candle on its miner’s cap.

![[mechadrons-category.webp|right|414x555]] Beings of magic and machinery, [[mechadrons]] embody absolute law. These inhabitants of the clockwork plane of Mechanus tend to the incredible mechanisms of their orderly home and oppose chaotic forces across the multiverse.

Mechadrons are parts of a vast hierarchy, spanning from quirky solodrones to the leader of their kind, the godlike Primus. Every mechadron carries out tasks assigned to it by higher-ranking mechadrons, doing so without question. Generally, mechadrons communicate only with other mechadrons of their own rank or the ranks immediately above and below them. Those more than one rank away tend to be either too advanced or too simple for them to understand.

Mechadrons excel at tasks that require patience and precision, but they have little understanding of nonliteral concepts such as art or humor. They have no egos; they have only their work and the certainty that multiversal law depends on their efficacy.

In rare cases, a mechadron goes rogue and develops its own will. In these cases, other mechadrons are sent to recover or destroy their malfunctioning kin.

**Planar Constructs**. Mechadrons are living [[Construct|constructs]] that inhabit the outer planes of law and order. All mechadrons are manufactured by the mechadrons that came before. Each is crafted for a specific purpose, and mechadrons work in unison to accomplish seemingly impossible tasks.

**We of One**. All mechadrons refer to themselves as “we” and seem intrinsically drawn to one another. No one knows if mechadrons even contain a sense of self or desire outside of “The Plan,” a goal they all serve.

**Machine Speech Relay**. A mechadron can relay communications between other mechadrons using a specialized, high-pitched dialect of [[Machine Speech]]. This helps larger mechadrons coordinate attacks, marches, ambushes, or looting operations. Mechadrons can also relay Machine Speech with one another in line of sight using nearly imperceptible eye movements. They transfer vast amounts of information in seconds this way. Sometimes, parts of their Machine Speech break into [[Common]], leaving listeners with tidbits of their conversation.

**Reconfiguration**. Mechadrons don’t evolve, grow, or change on their own. However, they can combine with a few hours of work, reconfiguring themselves for greater effectiveness and potency. For instance, a solodron that combines with a dupladron becomes a tripladron. Through reconfiguration, mechadrons drastically change their demeanor, skill, and purpose. Through great effort and planning, a mechadron can reconfigure itself back into its original components, though this is rare.

**Solodron**. The [[Mechadron, Solodron|solodron]] is a fast-flying surveyor and scout. From a mile away, it can judge measurements and distances to within a fraction of an inch, or one thousandth of a quantant, the universal unit of measure that all mechadrons use, without offering any conversion for other creatures. Roughly speaking, a quantant is about 1.5 feet.

**Dupladron**. Heavily crafted, the dupladron tirelessly works to reshape its environment in whatever way the Plan instructs. The dupladron relies on solodron scouts to ensure precise positioning of each structure. Capable of creating or demolishing sturdy structures in a few hours or days, solodrons can even dig trenches in minutes, providing shelter for themselves, companions, or plunder.

**Tripladron**. Sturdy and weaponized, tripladrons function as field troops and repairers, defending and repairing other mechadrons in the field. Their bodies contain a variety of arms tipped with different implements, such as grabbing pincers, drills, slicing blades, and small hammers, all of which can be used alternately for repairs or in defense of their fellow mechadrons.

**Tetradron**. The strongest and most intelligent of the mechadrons, the tetradron’s mechanical wings and helmet-like head make it look almost angelic beside other mechadrons. Tetradrons are the core carriers of the Plan, and their precise leadership ensures each mechadron does its part to enact the Plan. Tetradrons protect other mechadrons, especially from living creatures, but all mechadrons understand the importance of the tetradrons’ role, sacrificing themselves to protect the carriers of the Plan at all costs. If the worst should come to pass and a tetradron is destroyed, the surviving mechadrons gather what remains of the tetradron and reconfigure themselves into a new tetradron, absorbing the slain tetradron’s knowledge and continuing to enact the directives of the Plan.
## Mechadron Marches
Whether in service to lawful deities or as part of the Great Mechadron March, mechadrons travel from Mechanus to spread their vision of law to other planes of existence.
