# GM Screen

A solo-play GM Screen. It **assigns** GM tools from `gm-tools/` (in the
`gm-tools:` frontmatter above) and surfaces them as cards on the GM side of any
session log that links this file via `gm: "[[00. GM Screen]]"`. Each assigned
tool's `rpg feature.gm` blocks become card stacks; open a tool's own page to
read how it works and play with its example.

## Assigned tools

- **Dial:** [[Chaos Factor]] — the session's tension dial (default 5). `[CF+1]` / `[CF-1]` move it; every oracle and scene test reads its current value.
- **Oracle:** [[Fate Chart|Fate Question]] (d100, odds picked per question), [[NPC Behavior]] (expectation, Fate Question, or a Meaning Table). Doubles at or under the Chaos Factor flag a Random Event.
- **Meaning:** [[Discover Meaning]] (Actions, Descriptions or Elements), plus a roll on any one Elements table.
- **List:** [[Threads]] and [[Characters]] — roll on either; grown and pruned with `[Threads+ …]` / `[Characters- …]`.
- **Combat:** [[Initiative|Start Combat]] — add the foes, pick who in the party fights, and roll everyone's initiative.
- **Generator:** [[Name Generator]] — pick a naming style (Anglish, Nörslik, Elven…) and roll its Nomicon tables for people, places and settlements.
- **Scene:** [[Scene Check|New Scene]] (move the Chaos Factor, then test the expected scene — an Altered or Interrupt result continues into its steps), [[Random Event]], [[First Scene]], and [[Bookkeeping|End Scene]] (update the Lists).

Add more by dropping a tool page into a `gm-tools/<type>/` folder and listing it
under `gm-tools:`.
