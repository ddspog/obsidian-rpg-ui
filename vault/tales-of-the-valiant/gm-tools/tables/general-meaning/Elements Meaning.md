#### Elements Meaning Tables
The Elements Meaning Tables are themed giving more targeted results. The most general Element Meaning Tables are Location, Character, and Object; the Element Meaning Tables in the "Scenes" chapter are even more specialized with options such as Character Personality, Scavenging Results, and Dungeon Traps.
