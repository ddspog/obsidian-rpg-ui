## Understanding Challenge Rating
```rpg rule.side
title: Go easy at 1st Level
type: rules
---
1st level PCs are more vulnerable than PCs at any other level in the game. When your players are just getting started, be careful with the monsters you choose in combat encounters. The best ways to avoid outright casualties at 1st level are sticking with monsters no higher than CR 1/4 and using fewer monsters than there are PCs. Monsters of CRs higher than 1/2 can often inflict enough damage to drop (or outright kill) a 1st level PC in a single turn. Be nice at 1st level. You have the whole rest of their adventuring career to bring the pain.
```
Every creature in this book has a challenge rating (CR) from 0 to 30 with fractional challenge ratings for low‑challenge creatures like 1/8, 1/4, and 1/2. This challenge rating describes the relative difficulty of a creature when compared to other creatures. A CR 2 creature is less difficult than a CR 4 which is less difficult than a CR 8. This challenge rating also helps determine the potential difficulty of a combat encounter when comparing groups of monsters to groups of PCs. 

Depending on the average level of the PCs, creatures within a range of CRs tend to work better than those outside of this range. If a creature’s CR is too low, it provides very little challenge. Too high, and the creature can be frustrating to fight or even outright deadly to the PCs. The **PC Level to Creature CR** table shows the range of creature CRs that tend to match well against PCs of a given level. The lower number represents the lowest CR likely to challenge the PCs (often in large numbers of monsters), while the higher number represents the highest single creature CR the PCs can likely defeat in combat.
```rpg table.pc-level-to-creature-cr
|PC LEVEL|SUITABLE CREATURE CRS|
|---|---|
|1|0–1/2|
|2–4|1/8–6|
|5–10|1–15|
|11–16|3–21|
|17–20|5–30|
[PC LEVEL TO CREATURE CR]
```
Don’t shy away from using lower CR creatures than those listed if it makes sense for the situation. They might not prove much of a challenge but can still make sense given the story of the adventure. Plus, hewing through the occasional swarm of minions can be a fun shakeup for the PCs!
