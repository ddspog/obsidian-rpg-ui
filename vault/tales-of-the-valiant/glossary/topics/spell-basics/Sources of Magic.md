## Sources of Magic
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
All magic requires energy. The four sources of magic represent the four types of cosmic energy and each fuels the abilities of different spellcasters. Every time a caster produces a spell, they use their personal ability to draw energy from one of the four sources of magic: [[Arcane]], [[Divine]], [[Primordial]], and [[Wyrd]]. This section explains each one and its role in spellcasting. 
```
