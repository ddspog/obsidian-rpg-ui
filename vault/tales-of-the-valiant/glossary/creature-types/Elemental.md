## Elementals
These creatures born of raw elemental chaos can be [[humanoid]] or [[beast|bestial]] in shape, although true elementals, including the creatures simply called elementals, have no corporeal body beyond the elements from which they formed. [[Elemental|Elementals]] include beings like azers, genies, invisible stalkers, and salamanders. 

**_True Elementals._** A “true” elemental is an animate mass of its origin element, such as an air elemental whose form is howling dust-filled wind or an earth elemental comprised completely of mud and stone. True elementals dissipate into unbound elemental magic when destroyed.

### Allies
[[Elemental|Elementals]] are most comfortable in areas that match their element and typically ally with creatures also comfortable in those environments. This means most Elementals ally with other Elementals, though many Elementals still find allies elsewhere, such as the azer’s kinship with dwarves through a mutual love of crafting. Regardless of common ties, allies of Elementals almost always include creatures accustomed to exposure to the elements of their allied Elementals, preventing pain or even lasting harm to the ally. While humanoid and bestial elementals like genies or salamanders may create sprawling communities or have ties to various allies, true elementals are almost always found inexorably tied to the will of more intelligent Elementals or to spellcasters that have summoned them or otherwise bound them to service. 
### Traits and Themes
[[Elemental|Elementals]] are infused or made of an element, and so their physiology is impervious to that element. Similarly, their bodies are adapted to harsh environments where their element is prevalent. For example, fire-based Elementals often have hair and hide incapable of catching fire, water-based Elementals often have thick hide that retains moisture, and earth-based elementals often have iron-hard bones or sandy or tar-like blood. 
### Encounter Locations 
[[Elemental|Elementals]] rarely appear on the [[Material Plane]], though powerful elemental upheavals may create areas that spontaneously give rise to them (a tsunami might spawn a water elemental, for instance). Elementals gravitate to natural locations where their element is most common and use their affinity for that environment to defend themselves, inhabiting the darkest depths of the ocean, swimming through pools of lava, or racing the winds through a mountainous ravine.
