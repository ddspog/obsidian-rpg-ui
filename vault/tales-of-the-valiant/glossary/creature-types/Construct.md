## Constructs
[[Construct|Constructs]] are made, not born. Often built of metal, stone, or wood, some are charged by their creators to follow a simple set of instructions, while others are given sentience and think independently. Golems, mechadrons, and clockwork creatures are iconic Constructs. 

**_Golem Tag._** [[Golem|Golems]] are constructs that are almost always [[humanoid]] in shape, are typically crafted wholly out of a specific material, and have some magical connection to their creators. This connection might be that the golem was made from the remains of the creator’s loved one, made with some of the creator’s blood, made from an object with great sentimental significance to the creator, or made with some other object or material personal to the creator.

### Allies
A [[Construct]] is a created being often with specific instructions imprinted into its consciousness during creation. As such, Constructs rarely choose their allies, at least initially. Constructs that still serve their creators are typically allied with those creators, their creator’s allies and servitors, and other like-minded creatures. Constructs that no longer serve their creators—often due to the creator’s death or imprisonment—might ally with creatures similar to their creators or with creatures serving the creator’s goals. Constructs created for a specific purpose then left to it by their creators, such as a Construct instructed to guard a location, ally with other creatures that align with that purpose, such as aiding and defending creatures in the guarded location. 
### Traits and Themes
[[Construct|Constructs]] can come in all shapes, sizes, and body styles as suited for the Constructs’ primary purposes. Most Constructs have limited intelligence and devote themselves to endlessly carrying out their original directives—often over decades or even centuries—even if the current situation makes such directives out-of-date, irrelevant, or even dangerous. Constructs whose creators have long since perished might develop different quirks over the years, such as a fondness for a particular flower that grows in the locale the Construct inhabits or a protectiveness toward creatures that wear their creator’s favorite color. 
### Encounter Locations
[[Construct|Constructs]] serve, aid, or protect specific people or locations, especially people and locations of magical significance, like an archmage or a temple. Because Constructs can often outlive their creators by centuries, Constructs are typically found in ancient ruins, hidden treasuries, forgotten wizard towers, in service to a monarch’s bloodline, or even as seemingly decorative statues that remain motionless along a city’s walls for centuries. Such inactive Constructs might awaken only when the city is in grave danger—or to clean the palace once a decade.
