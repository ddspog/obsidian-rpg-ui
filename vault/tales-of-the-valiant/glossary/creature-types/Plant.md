## Plants
![[shrieker.webp|right|384]] [[Plant|Plants]] are vegetable creatures, not ordinary flora. Most are ambulatory, some are carnivorous, and many are sentient. Most Plants are naturally occurring creatures on the [[Material Plane]], while some gain sentience through druidic magic or planar influence in parts of the world where the veil between planes is thin. The quintessential Plants are the shambling mound and the treant. Fungal creatures, such as shriekers and mycolids, also fall into this category.

### Allies
Due to [[Fey]]’s innate connection with the natural world, [[Plant|Plants]] often ally with Fey that inhabit the same areas, defending or working alongside those Fey to protect their homes. Plants also tend to ally with [[Beast|Beasts]], friendly [[Monstrosity|Monstrosities]], and other Plants, creating symbiotic relationships or communities with those other creatures. Rarely, Plants can be cultivated as engines of war by [[Humanoid|Humanoids]], tasked with collapsing walls and boring through defenders with their gnarled roots. 
### Traits and Themes
Creatures of the [[Plant]] type tend to be primarily comprised of vegetation or share numerous traits with mundane plants. Some Plants have characteristics in common with [[Beast|Beasts]] or [[Humanoid|Humanoids]] or even have bestial or human‑like shapes, but these are still comprised primarily of vegetation like other Plants. However, most Plants look identical or very similar to mundane plants of a similar type when they remain completely motionless, allowing them to hide from predators and ambush prey. Plant physiology and their mobility also make them hardier and less susceptible to changes in their environments when compared to mundane plants. 
### Encounter Locations
[[Plant|Plants]] are found in thickly overgrown areas above and below ground, such as glades, forests, grasslands, and fungal-infested caverns. Only rarely are Plants found in urban regions unless they are laying siege to entrenched enemies on behalf of [[Fey]] or [[Humanoid|Humanoids]]. Fungal Plants tend to prefer dim, dark, or damp areas, like caves, sewers, and the deepest forests and rainforests.
