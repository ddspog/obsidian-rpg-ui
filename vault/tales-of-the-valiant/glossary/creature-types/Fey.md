## Fey
[[Fey]] are magical creatures bound to the forces of nature itself. They dwell in twilight groves, misty forests, and in the unnoticed places surrounding civilization. Many Fey are enigmatic, ageless, and consumed with the need to collect and exchange secrets, though not all of them; some more harmless Fey spend their time playing pranks on [[Humanoid|Humanoids]] or tending to plants in blighted regions. In some worlds, Fey are closely tied to other planes of existence, such as the Plane of Fey or the Plane of Shadow, where they may pursue darker flights of fancy beyond the watchful eyes of powerful archfey. Fey include dryads, hags, and satyrs.

### Allies
Most [[Fey]], even the unassuming blink dog, possess human‑like intelligence. These clever beings often form close-knit symbiotic bonds with other local Fey and [[Beast|Beasts]], particularly those who safeguard the natural world, to protect one another. Many benevolent Fey are treated as minor gods by other creatures and sometimes even worshiped or served by cults of nature-focused [[Humanoid|Humanoids]]. Powerful malicious Fey, like hags and selangs, often ensnare desperate Humanoids into service with ironclad contracts or mind-wrenching enchantments, while lesser Fey may serve such powerful creatures through fear alone. 
### Traits and Themes
[[Fey]] are irrevocably tied to the magic inherent in nature, and most have a bit of innate magic. Regardless of a Fey’s plane of origin, they share common characteristics with [[Humanoid|Humanoids]] and [[Beast|Beasts]] from the [[Material Plane]], though these characteristics are often magnified or blended in unnatural ways. Some fey might be humanoid in every way except for their insectoid wings or flowering hair, some may have hooves or furred ears, and others still might appear completely human but can magically disguise or change their forms. 

**_Bountiful Lives_.** Many Fey have extremely long lifespans, due to their connection to nature. The more powerful a Fey is, the longer its life may become. Some archfey, considered the most powerful of all Fey beings, have lived for millennia and may live until the end of time. Even death itself is considered a season to most Fey, and should a Fey become powerful enough, death may pose little obstacle to their goals or ambitions. 
### Encounter Locations
[[Fey]] are almost always encountered in a natural setting. Misty forests, overgrown bogs, ancient ruins reclaimed by desert sands, or the island-strewn ocean beneath an endless downpour are all hospitable homes to many Fey. Some Fey are adapted to urban environments and can take [[Humanoid]] forms, hide in darkness, or beguile Humanoids to keep their presence secret. Incredibly powerful Fey may even rule their own safeguarded demiplanes, where they command lesser Fey and shape their surroundings with a thought.
