## Oozes
[[Ooze|Oozes]] are gelatinous predators without fixed forms, and they are eating machines. Often completely mindless, they consume anything they can dissolve with their bodies’ natural acids, living or otherwise. Mostly subterranean, scouring caves and dungeons for refuse, Oozes are happy to devour any living creatures unlucky enough to get in their way. Black puddings and gelatinous cubes are among the most recognizable Oozes.

### Allies
[[Ooze|Oozes]] largely lack the sapience required to forge lasting bonds. Content to squeeze through tiny gaps and feed on moss, detritus, and corpses, Oozes are considered vermin by some and share a vermin’s sensibilities. If coaxed into a trap via a trail of food or similar guile, Oozes might willingly remain inside the trap for days, weeks, or years, waiting for their next meal to happen by. Many clever creatures use captured Oozes this way for waste disposal, as mindless guards, and as living traps. Though not allies in the strictest sense, Oozes are occasionally accompanied by ghosts, specters, or skeletons, as the remnants of Oozes’ victims linger near them after death.  
### Traits and Themes
[[Ooze|Oozes]] are semisolid at best and often amorphous. Though some rare Oozes may possess near-human or higher intelligence, the majority of Oozes lack any type of mental ability beyond what is required to move toward their next meal. Many Oozes can remain perfectly still for unnaturally long periods and protrude parts of their body, called pseudopods, to attack and draw in prey. Other Oozes simply move inexorably toward something they believe they might consume with reckless abandon. Most Oozes naturally dissolve any creature they touch, making them dangerous to those they ambush or that can’t outpace them. 
### Encounter Locations
Most [[Ooze|Oozes]] can be found below ground and in fetid, inhospitable places. Crumbling corridors of ancient cities may be swarmed with Oozes feasting on rotting tapestries and wood, while lightless tunnels of sprawling dungeons may contain numerous Oozes that devour fresh corpses. In extremely rare cases, Oozes can be found in cities or towns, particularly in basements, catacombs, or sewers, where tiny vermin and hapless citizens might feed an Ooze indefinitely.
