## Aberration
[[Aberration|Aberrations]] are utterly alien beings with otherworldly senses, features, and minds. Many have innate magical abilities drawn from their alien mind rather than the mystical forces of the world. Though not all Aberrations are [[Outsider|outsiders]], their physiology is unusual and often entirely outside the bounds of the natural reality of the [[Material Plane]]. Even Aberrations native to the Material Plane often find the natural world chafing for one reason or another, as their physiology doesn’t quite mesh well with the natural world. Quintessential Aberrations are creatures like aboleths, chuul, and gibbering mouthers.

### Allies
Due to the alien and often extraplanar nature of most [[Aberration|Aberrations]], they work best with creatures that are better able to interact with and manipulate the material world. Sometimes this means allies that help with physical interactions with the world itself, though often it may mean social interactions with its inhabitants who would otherwise by terrified by the Aberration. 

Because Aberrations are often innately magical, they frequently choose nonmagical allies. This helps them avoid the risk of their own magic being resisted, countered, or otherwise disrupted. 
### Traits and Themes
The key feature of [[Aberration|Aberrations]] is their otherworldly physiology. This often includes powerful minds, writhing tentacles, and extra body parts, such as eyes, ears, and mouths. Many Aberrations scheme and manipulate others to gain power, some seek to bring the chaos of the Void to the [[Material Plane]], and others are trapped on the Material Plane and simply strive to exist comfortably. Whatever the case, an Aberration’s existence is often at odds with the natural workings of the world, causing the Aberration near-constant discomfort or causing the natural world to shun it, reality to warp around it, or nature to otherwise move away from it. 
### Encounter Locations 
Most [[Aberration|Aberrations]] aren’t naturally part of the [[Material Plane]] and end up there due to an accident, as part of a plan to hunt creatures or sow chaos, as the result of a magical catastrophe or anomaly, or a similar translocation. Because of this, Aberrations are typically part of a larger scheme or plot. They might be the masterminds behind a decades‑long scheme of disruption, they might seek magical power in places where the veil between the Void and the Material Plane is thin, or they might seek to transform native creatures into Aberrations. In places where powerful magic tore the fabric of reality, Aberrations might pour through in great numbers in pursuit of outright conquest.
