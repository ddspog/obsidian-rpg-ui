## Monstrosities
![[advanced-grick.webp|right|384]] These frightening, abnormal, unnatural creatures are almost never benign. Some [[Monstrosity|monstrosities]] are the result of magical experimentation gone awry (such as owlbears), others are the product of terrible curses (such as chimeras), and many are monstrous animals that dominate their natural habitats (such as ankhegs). Creatures that defy exact categorization and don’t fit within another type are ideal examples of the [[Monstrosity]] creature type. 

**_Animal Tag._** [[Animal|Animals]] are Monstrosities (and sometimes other creatures) that share enough characteristics with [[Beast|Beasts]] that they can be affected by spells, features, and other abilities that specifically target Beasts, such as the [[speak with animals]] spell. Ankhegs and owlbears are Monstrosities with the Animal tag.

### Allies
[[Monstrosity|Monstrosities]] often share characteristics with [[Beast|Beasts]] or [[Humanoid|Humanoids]], and many Monstrosities ally with such creatures. For example, the spider-like ettercap frequently allies with spiders. However, the oddness of Monstrosities makes many Beasts afraid of them and Humanoids wary of them, leading most Monstrosities to ally primarily with others of their kind. Intelligent Monstrosities often use their natural or magical abilities to influence or manipulate other creatures into serving them, while less intelligent monstrosities are frequently tamed or trained by Humanoids for use as mounts or guardians.  
### Traits and Themes
[[Monstrosity|Monstrosities]] are known for having frightening, unnatural, or unsettling characteristics that other creatures, especially [[Beast|Beasts]] or [[Humanoid|Humanoids]], find disturbing. Some blend humanoid and animal traits, others blend multiple animals, and a few possess a magical, particularly dangerous characteristic that clearly sets them apart from other similar animals. Because many Monstrosities are the results of magical experimentation or curses, they often possess magical features, such as breath weapons, magical stares, or even the ability to cast spells, and their bestial forms often belie a shrewd intelligence. 
### Encounter Locations
[[Monstrosity|Monstrosities]]’ unique bodies make them tougher than most other creatures, and many Monstrosities inhabit less‑hospitable environments because of this. Most Monstrosities favor remote, secluded, or well‑sheltered locations like caves, mines, or forlorn sea shores, but a Monstrosity claims territory or makes a home anywhere it can find food, shelter, and safety. While the small cockatrice might claim a copse of trees as its territory, the titanic kraken may lay claim to an area larger than most [[Humanoid]] kingdoms.
