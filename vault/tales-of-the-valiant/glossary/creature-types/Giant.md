## Giants
[[Giant|Giants]] are enormous, powerful beings that tower over most creatures. These titanic creatures are humanlike in shape, with unique characteristics that help them thrive in biting blizzards, roiling volcanos, raging storms, and other brutal landscapes. Giants have long memories and have existed in one form or another since the formation of the material realm. True giants include stone giants, fire giants, and cloud giants, but this creature type also includes ogres, ettins, and trolls.

### Allies
[[Giant|Giants]] mostly associate with other Giants of similar power as well as [[Humanoid|Humanoids]] who might worship or revere them. Giants tend to nurture local Beasts and [[Monstrosity|Monstrosities]] as pets, livestock, or beasts of burden. Occasionally, Giants seek companionship from such creatures or use them in hunting. For example, some frost giant families work with packs of winter wolves in ways that are mutually beneficial for the growth of both family and pack. Giants in particularly harsh or inhospitable environments, such as cloud or fire giants, ally with the few creatures found in those environments, such as air elementals, salamanders, or giant eagles.  
### Traits and Themes
[[Giant|Giants]] are all far larger than [[Humanoid|Humanoids]] and possess enormous strength. While true giants resemble humans, some giants, such as ettins and trolls, grow elongated limbs, additional heads, or excess appendages. The most powerful giants are so enormous that they can hold an adult human in their outstretched hand. Despite these differences, most Giants have societies, villages, family units, and cities just like many Humanoids, and like many Humanoids, they have their own laws and customs unique to each settlement or society. 
### Encounter Locations
True [[Giant|giants]] are reclusive and tend to be found around homelands matching their name. Giants with an elemental affinity, such as fire or frost giants, are typically found in environments that strongly feature that element. Giants that regularly trade with [[Humanoid|Humanoids]] can often be found near Humanoid settlements. Due to their size, all Giants are rarely willing to stay in areas where they can’t freely move, and elementally aligned Giants are particularly hesitant or even hostile when asked to enter a smaller area devoid of their element.
