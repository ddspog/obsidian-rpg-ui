## Celestial
[[Celestial|Celestials]] dwell in higher realms outside of measurable or material reality, often called heavens or paradise worlds. Many are servants of divine powers, serving as messengers or agents in the mortal realm and throughout the planes. Most of them lean innately toward forces of good and order. All of them strive to oppose or destroy [[Fiend|Fiends]]. Celestials include beings like angels, couatls, and unicorns. 

**_Angel Tag_.** Angels are [[Outsider]] Celestials that are direct agents of good deities or operate on behalf of such deities. Some are even created by deities for specific tasks.

### Allies
As agents of deities, goodness, or some other higher power, [[Celestial|Celestials]] ally with similarly motivated creatures. A Celestial might aid a paladin on a mission from their shared deity, might form a temporary alliance with a creature seeking assistance to correct a great injustice, or might arrive at a pivotal moment in a battle to help a creature with a greater destiny only the Celestial understands. In many cases, the recipient of the Celestial’s aid must prove worthiness—whether or not that recipient is even aware of the Celestial’s tests. As otherworldly creatures, Celestials have their own opinions on what might be worth their aid, and they view the mortal realm with an immortal gaze. Their aid, whether earned or withheld, often has some greater purpose unfathomable to the mortal mind. 

Whatever the case for their aid, Celestials that seek non‑Celestial allies typically align themselves with [[Humanoid|Humanoids]]. Celestials are almost never found allied with [[Fiend|Fiends]], malicious creatures, or agents of foul deities.  

When a Celestial aids such a creature, it is either at the behest of some unknowable greater plan, or, more often, the Celestial was manipulated into believing the malicious creature was just and upstanding. 
### Traits and Themes
Many [[Celestial|Celestials]] are creatures of light and goodness, both in ideals and in physicality. They often shed light, create radiance, promote life and growth, and similarly brighten or empower the world and creatures around them. Even Celestials that aren’t angels often have angelic qualities, such as melodic voices, feathered wings, shining fur, glowing eyes, metallic skin, or similar. Though many Celestials have human-like forms and mannerisms, just as many are completely alien in appearance with bodies of holy fire, bodies covered in dozens of eyes in exacting formations, bodies made of concentric rings that emit an otherworldly melody, or bodies made up of other variations that seem bizarre to Humanoids, such as pure light or feathered serpents.  
### Encounter Locations
Many [[Celestial|Celestials]] aren’t naturally from the [[Material Plane]] and visit only on specific missions with particular goals in mind. Because of this, Celestials encountered on the Material Plane are usually protecting or watching over sites dedicated to deities of light and goodness, quietly observing locales where events of cosmic significance are taking place, or encouraging mortals to rise up against tyranny and oppression.
