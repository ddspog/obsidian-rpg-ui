## Dragons
As large, reptilian creatures of ancient origin and tremendous power, [[Dragon|Dragons]] are both legendary and feared. True dragons are highly intelligent, have innate magic, and grow in power as they increase in age, called age categories. This creature type also includes creatures distantly related to true dragons, but less powerful, less magical, and often less intelligent, such as wyverns and pseudodragons. 

**_Drakes and Dragonettes._** Drakes are a bit more “primitive” than their cousins—less evolved physically, intellectually, or magically as true dragons. Dragonettes are also not quite as evolved as their true dragon cousins, and they usually have a connection with [[Humanoid|Humanoids]], often through bonding with spellcasters. Dragonettes are sometimes even magically bred by Humanoids for specific purposes or roles in Humanoid societies. 

In a pale mirror of their true dragon cousins’ affinity for specific treasures, drakes, dragonettes, and other dragons tend to have some type of theme or area of interest that defines them, such as the scorch drake’s passion for collecting charred bones or the pseudodragon’s curiosity about magical Humanoids.

### Allies
As apex predators and powerful beings in general, [[Dragon|dragons]], especially true dragons, view most other creatures as beneath them, leading alliances to be based on subjugation, willing service to the dragon, worship of the dragon, or out of the dragon’s desire to help or protect the “lesser” creatures. Even the most benevolent true dragons see themselves as separate from those they aid. Most other types of dragons tend to be solitary, content with hunting prey and collecting the target of their obsession. Dragonettes are one of the few exceptions, preferring to ally with—and even serve—[[Humanoid|Humanoids]], especially Humanoids with similar interests to the dragonette’s obsession. 
### Traits and Themes
[[Dragon|Dragons]] are scaly beasts often with breath weapons or similarly powerful exhalations, but they are otherwise as varied as the environments in which they are found. Most Dragons are uniquely adapted to their home environments, and dragons have lasting and often subtle impacts on their surroundings. The Dragon’s chosen prey or its obsession shapes the territory around it, because the Dragon maintains that territory to expand its prey or to produce more of its object. In some cases, a Dragon strips territory of everything and moves on, but many prefer to tend their territory over many years. 

**_Draconic Hoards._** All [[Dragon|Dragons]] create hoards or are keenly interested in a particular thing. True dragons have preferences based on the type of dragon they are, and their hoards contain a wide variety of similar items (all golden, all iron, or all magical, for instance). Drakes and dragonettes are much more focused in their obsessions, collecting only very specific things. A silver dragon might collect histories of various peoples, including texts, coins, statues, and similar aspects of those peoples, while a pseudodragon might covet just the hair ties used by a particular people or even treasure the people themselves, seeing their hoard as the number of friendships they can acquire and maintain. 
### Encounter Locations
True [[Dragon|dragons]] are most often encountered in their lairs, though true dragons that enjoy meeting [[Humanoid|Humanoids]] or meddling in Humanoid affairs might be found disguised in the courts of kings, hidden among villagers at a festival, or excitedly observing a bustling city from a cliff above it. Drakes and other more instinct-driven Dragons are usually encountered while hunting prey or indulging in their chosen obsession. Because dragonettes are very attached to Humanoids and Humanoid societies, they can be found perched on the shoulders of Humanoids, delivering messages across a city, or sleeping on the counter of a village shop.
