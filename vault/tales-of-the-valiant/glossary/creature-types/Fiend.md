## Fiends
[[Fiend|Fiends]] dwell in lower realms outside of measurable or material reality. A few are servants of deities, but many more labor under the leadership of archdevils and demon princes. Priests and mages sometimes summon Fiends to the material world and bind them into service. Most Fiends lean innately toward forces of evil and chaos, and all true Fiends strive to oppose or destroy [[Celestial|Celestials]]. Fiends include [[Outsider|demons]], [[Outsider|devils]], hell hounds, and rakshasas. 

**_Demon Tag._** Fiends from the chaotic areas of the Lower Planes, demons are typically more chaotic. They are destructive and volatile and rarely stick to plans or goals for long, because they easily become bored without the constant guidance from a superior demon. Because they tend to recognize and respect only power, Demon Lords rule weaker demons, and these lesser demons constantly squabble among themselves for supremacy and rule over demons even weaker than themselves. 

**_Devil Tag._** Fiends from the orderly areas of the Lower Planes, devils are more organized and lawful than demons. They are corruptive and malicious, endlessly toiling to amass power, prestige, and mortal souls. They work in strict hierarchies with Arch Devils at the top and other devils ruling each other in perfectly-structured tiers, often based on each devil’s individual accomplishments or influence.

### Allies
Fiends seldom trust anyone, mortal or immortal, but they often strike temporary alliances to accomplish specific tasks or goals. A [[fiend]] might aid a mortal creature with a similar goal or seek to coerce a mortal into helping it with its current task. As mortal souls, particularly [[Humanoid]] souls, are currency and even food in some parts of the Lower Planes, most fiends helping or working with mortals have ulterior motives. Indeed, they seek to capture that soul, either by killing the mortal once the initial task is accomplished or otherwise by corrupting the mortal, so that its soul goes to the Lower Planes when it dies. Otherworldly and immortal, Fiends are rarely obvious about such motives, and many seem quite helpful, offering assistance again and again but always with the end goal of gaining that mortal’s soul when it dies. 

Because of the value of Humanoid souls, Fiends ally themselves with Humanoids, even if the task on the [[Material Plane]] is trivial for the Fiend. On occasion, a Fiend allies with other Fiends or non-Humanoids, but this is typically done only at the behest of a superior. 
### Traits and Themes
[[Fiend|Fiends]] of all types are native to harsh, inhospitable planes filled with creatures, hazards, and threats that many mortals only experience in nightmares. Most Fiends are comfortable amid rot and disease, and some even produce deadly poisons from their fangs, tails, or natural gases. Fiends often have spines, horns, leathery skin, sharp teeth, vicious claws, or spear-like tails, and some have a natural resistance to magic. Though many Fiends have human-like or animal-like forms, just as many are completely alien in appearance with forms and features as nightmarish and terrifying as the places they call home. 
### Encounter Locations
Many [[Fiend|Fiends]] arrive on the [[Material Plane]] because they are summoned or magically bound to a specific location or person. As a result, Fiends on the Material Plane are often protecting the temples of evil deities, patrolling the holdings of spellcasters, or directly serving powerful [[Humanoid|Humanoids]] as guards or assassins. Regardless of their reason for being on the Material Plane, Fiends try to subvert any service they are bound to provide or try to slowly corrupt any mortals they encounter. They often interpret the terms of their service literally and stretch the limits of their bindings as far as they possibly can.
