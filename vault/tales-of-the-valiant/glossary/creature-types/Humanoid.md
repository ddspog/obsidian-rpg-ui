## Humanoids
[[Humanoid|Humanoids]] are usually bipedal and warm-blooded, including humans and most other lineages. Humanoids are notoriously adaptable; varied in talent, power, and skill; and capable of drastic change. The most common Humanoids are the lineages available to player characters, including [[Human|humans]], dwarves, [[Elf|elves]], and halflings. Almost as numerous are beings like goblins, gnolls, and lizardfolk.

### Allies
![[drow-mage.webp|right|384]] [[Humanoid|Humanoids]] understand their limitations and try to shore them through alliances, which could mean building guilds of like‑minded Humanoids, hiring a legion of mercenary fiends, magically summoning fey, raising undead, or investing countless hours training [[Beast|Beasts]] or building [[Construct|Constructs]]. Humanoids—whether driven by love, honor, greed, vengeance—can be a staunch ally with any other creature, making them a universal and powerful threat. 

Humanoid stat blocks in the [[Appendix, NPCs|NPC Appendix]] can be applied to any other type of Humanoid (for instance, the mage stats could be used for a human mage or lizardfolk mage). As such, those Humanoids don’t list allies in the table included here. Allies of Humanoids with NPC stat blocks are assumed to be the same allies of that base Humanoid, or NPCs are assumed to be allied with others of the same or similar type of NPC. For example, a lizardfolk mage is likely to be allied with other lizardfolk, black dragons, crocodiles, and flinderbeasts, just like any other lizardfolk. Similarly, a priest is likely to be allied with acolytes, cultists, and fanatic cultists, and they often match the Humanoid lineage chosen for that priest. 
### Traits and Themes
[[Humanoid|Humanoids]] share a typical appearance: two arms, two legs, a torso, and upright posture. While not as innately magical as [[Fey]] or as mundane as [[Beast|Beasts]], Humanoids are intelligent, capable of speech, and sometimes wield magic, and they gain that access through study, magic-infused blood, or by gift of some powerful being. Humanoids almost always live in groups or societies, creating their own cultures, laws, and traditions wherever they are found. Curiosity is a major driving factor behind the survival, tenacity, and growth of many Humanoid individuals and as whole societies. 
### Encounter Locations
[[Humanoid|Humanoids]] are highly adaptable and able to use all types of equipment, magic, and weapons to defend themselves. They can prosper almost anywhere, even hostile caverns, arctic wastelands, or the high seas. Some Humanoids possess specific traits that help them survive in these harsh conditions, such as the ability to see in the dark or breathe underwater. Humanoids tend to live near sources of water and food, in cities or frontier camps, and surrounded by others of their kind.
