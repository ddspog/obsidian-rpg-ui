# Your Chosen RPG
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
Mythic is meant to fill the role of Game Master for your role-playing adventures. It can be used with just about any RPG out there, allowing you to play the game while Mythic answers your questions just like a Game Master would.

For more on pairing Mythic with another game, see [[Using Mythic with Another RPG]] and [[Using Mythic with Another RPG#Using Fate Questions to Replace RPG Rules|replacing its rules with Fate Questions]].
```
