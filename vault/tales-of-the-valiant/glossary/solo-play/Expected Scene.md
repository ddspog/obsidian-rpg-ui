## Expected Scene
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
At the end of a scene you decide how you think the next one will start, usually based on what your PC plans to do next. That guess is the **Expected Scene**. You then run the [[Scene Check]]: roll over the [[Chaos Factor]] and it happens as expected; roll within it and it becomes an [[Altered Scene]] or [[Interrupt Scene]] instead.
```
