# Underwater Combat
Fighting underwater is a challenging environment with special rules to represent that challenge. 

When making a [[Melee Attack#Weapon Attacks|melee weapon attack]], a creature that doesn’t have a [[swimming|swimming speed]] (either natural or granted by magic) has [[disadvantage]] on attack rolls unless the weapon is a dagger, [[javelin]], [[shortsword]], spear, or trident. 

A [[Ranged Attack#Weapon Attacks|ranged weapon attack]] can’t hit a target beyond its normal range. The attack roll has [[disadvantage]] even within normal range unless the weapon is a crossbow, net, or a weapon that is thrown like a [[javelin]] (such as a spear, trident, or dart). 

Creatures and objects that are fully immersed in water have resistance to fire damage.
