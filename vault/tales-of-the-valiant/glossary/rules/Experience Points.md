# Experience Points
````rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
[[Experience points]] (XP) represent the knowledge a PC gains from completing tasks, fulfilling quest objectives, and a variety of other activities, including defeating creatures (see the _Player’s Guide_ for more information on XP and its impact on PCs). The number of experience points a creature is worth is based on its CR, as detailed in the Experience Points by Challenging Rating table. Typically, XP is awarded for defeating a creature, although the GM may also award XP for creatively avoiding or neutralizing the threat posed by the creature.
````
