# Long Jump
```rpg rule.content
name: Long Jump
---
When you make a long jump, you can cover a number of feet up to half your walking speed if you move at least 10 feet on foot immediately before the jump. When you make a standing long jump, you can leap only a quarter of your walking speed. Either way, each foot you clear on the jump costs 1 foot of movement. 

This rule assumes that the height of your jump doesn’t matter, such as a jump across a stream or chasm. At the GM’s option, you must succeed on a DC 10 STR ([[Athletics]]) check to clear a low obstacle (no taller than a quarter of the jump’s distance), such as a hedge or low wall. Otherwise, you hit it. 

When you attempt to land in [[difficult terrain]], you must succeed on a DC 10 DEX ([[Acrobatics]]) check to land on your feet. Otherwise, you [[prone|land prone]]. 
```
