kind: callout
type: rules
title: EQUIPMENT PACKS
---
The starting equipment you get from your class includes a collection of useful adventuring gear, put together in a pack. The contents of these packs are listed here. You can purchase a pack for the price shown, which might be cheaper than buying the items individually. 

**Burglar’s Pack (16 gp).** [[Backpack]], bag of 1,000 ball bearings, 10 feet of string, bell, 5 [[Candle]], [[Crowbar]], [[Hammer]], 10 [[Piton]], hooded lantern, 2 [[Oil (flask)]], 5 [[Rations (1 day)]], [[Tinderbox]], and [[Waterskin]]. The pack also has [[Rope (50 feet)]] strapped to the side. 
 
**Diplomat’s Pack (39 gp).** Chest, 2 [[Case, map or scroll]], set of [[Clothes, fine]], bottle of ink, ink pen, lamp, 2 [[Oil (flask)]], 5 [[Paper (one sheet)]], vial of perfume, sealing wax, and soap. 
 
**Dungeoneer’s Pack (12 gp).** [[Backpack]], [[Crowbar]], [[Hammer]], 10 [[Piton]], 10 [[Torch]], [[Tinderbox]], 10 [[Rations (1 day)]], and [[Waterskin]]. The pack also has [[Rope (50 feet)]] strapped to the side. 
 
**Entertainer’s Pack (40 gp).** [[Backpack]], [[Bedroll]], 2 costumes, 5 [[Candle]], 5 [[Rations (1 day)]] [[Waterskin]], and a variety of cosmetics. 
 
**Explorer’s Pack (10 gp).** [[Backpack]], [[Bedroll]], [[Mess Kit]], [[Tinderbox]], 10 [[Torch]], 10 [[Rations (1 day)]], and [[Waterskin]]. The pack also has [[Rope (50 feet)]] strapped to the side. 
 
**Priest’s Pack (19 gp).** [[Backpack]], [[Blanket]], 10 [[Candle]], [[Tinderbox]], [[Alms Box ᴺ]], 2 [[Incense (one block) ᴺ]], [[Censer ᴺ]], [[Vestments ᴺ]], 2 days of [[Rations (1 day)]], and [[Waterskin]]. 
 
**Scholar’s Pack (40 gp).** [[Backpack]], book of lore, bottle of ink, ink pen, 10 sheets of parchment, little bag of sand, and a small knife.
