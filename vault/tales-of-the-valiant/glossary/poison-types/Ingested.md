# INgested
```rpg rule.content
name: Contact
---
A creature must swallow a full dose of ingested poison to suffer its effects. The dose can be delivered in food or a liquid. You might decide that a partial dose has a reduced effect, such as having advantage on the save or dealing half damage on a failed save.
```
