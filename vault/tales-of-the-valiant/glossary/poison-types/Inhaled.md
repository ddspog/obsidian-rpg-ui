# Inhaled
```rpg rule.content
name: Inhaled
---
These poisons are powders or gases that take effect when breathed in. Blowing powder or releasing gas subjects creatures in a 5-foot cube to its effect. The resulting cloud dissipates immediately afterward. Holding one’s breath is ineffective against inhaled poisons, as they affect nasal membranes, tear ducts, and other parts of the body.
```
