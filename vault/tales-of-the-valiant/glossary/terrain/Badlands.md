# Badlands
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
The rocky, arid regions and wastelands of the world seem to attract strange creatures able to survive on little but air and carrion. [[Badlands]] terrain features towering, eroded rock, sparse vegetation, and infrequent rains. Badlands terrain also encompasses magic-blasted wastelands, which are characterized by magical storms, alchemical rivers, and mutated vegetation. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in badlands terrain include: 
- A rocky outcropping dotted with the burrows of lizards and poisonous snakes. 
- A magically blasted, sparsely covered forest filled with eerie lights and the void-infused music of the selang. 
- A “forest” of tall, eroded rocks that serve as the base of operations for a group of goblins and their worg companions. 
```
