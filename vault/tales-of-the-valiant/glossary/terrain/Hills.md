# Hills
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
Often called foothills or piedmont terrain, this is the hilly, elevated land near the base of mountains or mountain ranges. It often includes plateaus, gradual slopes, gullies or canyons, natural ridges, and small caves. [[Hills]] flora features shrubs, grasses, and sparse trees as the terrain shifts from grasslands to mountains, providing shelter for smaller fauna to hide from predators and enough space for larger fauna to freely roam. Hills terrain’s ridges and small caves also allow for mingling between surface and underground flora and fauna. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in hills terrain include: 
- A battle-scarred, humanoid fortress has been repurposed as a lair by an adult copper dragon. 
- A musty cave filled with oddly shaped stones is home to a mated pair of basilisks and their clutch of eggs. 
- A wood-walled hill giant settlement nestled in a small valley, along with their herd of enormous mountain goats. 
```
