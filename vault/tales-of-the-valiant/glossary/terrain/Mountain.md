# Mountain
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
The highest elevations of the world feature exposed bedrock and sharp valleys. [[Mountain]] life is shaped by the terrain’s elevation, with temperatures falling as elevation increases. While lower-elevation life resembles that found in hills, middle-elevation mountain life sees the most diversity with moderate temperatures and high rainfall encouraging dense forests and supporting a wide range of plants and monsters. High-elevation life requires adaptations to the low temperatures, drier climate, and short growing seasons. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in mountain terrain include: 
- Small, bubbling lava flows along a stony ridge that is home to a colony of magma mephits. 
- A snow-covered, ancient dwarven settlement carved into a mountainside serves as the lair of an adult silver dragon that remembers the dwarves of long ago. 
- A large circular arena worn smooth by generations of stone giant competitions in boulder-tossing, swrestling, rock carving, and sorcery. 
```
