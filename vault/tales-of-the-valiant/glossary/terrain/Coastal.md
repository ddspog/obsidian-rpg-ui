# Coastal
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
Where land meets water, along a freshwater lake or a storm‑driven ocean, marine life and land blend into unusual forms that are home along the shore. [[Coastal]] terrain includes land shaped by waves or shifting tides, as well as water shaping sandy beaches, sea caves, and rocky cliffs. A mix of salt and fresh water may shape salt marshes, seaweed forests, or tidal lagoons. Coastal life requires adaptations to surviving on land and in water, to high winds, and to rich nutrients, often carried by the water. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in coastal terrain include: 
- A sea hag and her sahuagin allies live in sea caves in a rocky cliffside, alternating dry and underwater as the tides shift. 
- A beach dotted with tidepools inhabited by [[Crab, Giant|giant crabs]] is the favored hunting ground of a young bronze dragon. Enormous cracked shells litter the beach. 
- A thriving coral reef where a local humanoid fishing village casts their nets daily and merfolk sing at high tide.  
```
