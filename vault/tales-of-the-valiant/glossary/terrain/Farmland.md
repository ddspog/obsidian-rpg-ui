# Farmland
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
Land used for crops or livestock is usually tended by [[Humanoid|humanoids]], and that land supports small villages and towns. It features cultivated cropland, crafted irrigation, and pastures for grazing livestock. [[Farmland]] life is characterized by being part of humanoid agriculture, such as labor animals, livestock animals, and edible plants, or by being adjacent to agriculture, such as pollinating fauna, livestock predators, and humanoid predators. Creatures found here are adapted to the presence of humanoids, thriving or stifled because of them. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in farmland include: 
- A small town cemetery inhabited by a flock of ravens and a few ghouls who feast on the town’s dead. 
- A large field of melon crops filled with the buzzing of wings as giant wasps devour the melons and drink the sugary insides. 
- An old farmhouse where a group of cultists meets with their fanatic cultist leader, who has a young goat bound and ready for sacrifice. 
```
