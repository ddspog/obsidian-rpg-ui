# Urban
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
All settlements featuring buildings, walls, fences, streets, and similar structures constitute [[urban]] terrain, typically in the form of cities, villages, or towns. Urban terrain includes secondary elements, like sewers, bridges, graveyards, and ruins of structures or older settlements. Similar to [[farmland]] terrain, urban terrain life is habituated to [[humanoid]] activities and influence. Urban flora is typically purposefully placed, such as gardens, fruit-bearing trees, or decorative bushes, and fauna is typically linked to humanoid life, such as pets, labor animals, or pest animals. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in urban terrain include: 
- A basement or attic laboratory where a master alchemist creates a flesh golem. 
- A musician’s academy filled with young bards and mage apprentices and secretly run by a virtuoso lich. 
- The armory of a king’s castle protected by dormant animated armors and flying swords. 
```
