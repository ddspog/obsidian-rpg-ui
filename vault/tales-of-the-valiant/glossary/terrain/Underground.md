# Underground
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
All forms of caverns, sinkholes, and subterranean spaces are [[underground]] terrain, including underground rivers, lakes, and waterfalls as well as any vast underworld of linked tunnels and caverns. While most of this is formed by nature, underground terrain can include [[humanoid]]‑made mines, tunnels, and dungeons. Underground life is adapted to limited resources and a lack of light, and fungi make up a prominent rung in the chain of life that sustains underground creatures. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in underground terrain include: 
- A collection of beetle-shaped, stony huts along a narrow stone passageway, indicating an outpost along a route to a drow settlement. 
- The [[ooze]]- and [[undead]]-filled dungeon beneath a lich’s tower. 
- A vast cavern filled with a towering, tree-like fungi forest tended by mycolids and home to giant bats.  
```
