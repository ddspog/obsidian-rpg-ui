# Swamp
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
[[Swamp]] terrain is any land that regularly floods or that has saturated soil or standing water. More importantly, it tends to be extremely fertile terrain, filled with wildlife that can feed enormous predators or support huge flocks of waterfowl or masses of croaking amphibians. Swamp terrain flourishes along rivers, deltas, and coasts as well as in areas with significant rainfall or groundwater such as rainforests or jungles. Swamp life is adapted to life on land and water, particularly stagnant water, which limits breathability and accumulates rich plant life alongside disease, parasites, and decaying plant matter. Aquatic and semi-aquatic flora and fauna dominate swamp terrain, and many fauna inhabit swamps seasonally or for certain life stages. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in swamp terrain include: 
- A green hag’s moss-covered hut at the base of a large cypress tree crawling with snakes and spiders. 
- A shallow, muddy lake sparkling with the light of fireflies and will-o’-wisps and where a wight and its zombie minions lie in wait to ambush lost travelers. 
- A copse of dogwood trees grown into the beginnings of circular “rooms” by a lairing young black dragon. 
```
