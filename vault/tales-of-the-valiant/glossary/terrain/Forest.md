# Forest
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
Any land dominated by trees, regardless of climate, is a [[forest]], from dry pinyon pineland to temperate oak forest to tropical jungle. The exact type of trees depends on temperature, the yearly rain, and soil drainage. Forest life features abundant plant and animal life that relies on the forest’s trees for food, perches, ambush sites, safe cover, and shelter from wind and weather. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in forest terrain include: 
- A logging camp on the edge of a temperate forest, where dryads and satyrs clash with logging humanoids. 
- An enchanted glade in the heart of an ancient forest inhabited by a unicorn and the circle of druids dedicated to protecting it. 
- A small, forest-covered cave that is home to an owlbear and its cubs. 
```
