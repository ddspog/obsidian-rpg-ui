# Planar
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
[[Planar]] terrain encompasses all realities and environments outside of the [[Material Plane]]. Planar life features the overarching aspect of that plane. For example, the Plane of Water is filled with life adapted to surviving in water, particularly magical water, while the Abyss is dominated by demons and other life adapted to ever-shifting chaos. In planes ruled by deities or other powerful, extraplanar entities, the native, non-sentient flora and fauna are often shaped by the whims of that entity, changing color, shape, temperament, and similar characteristics at a moment’s notice. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in planar terrain include: 
- A battlefield where armies of angels and devils clash— the battle is ending, and the wounded seek assistance. 
- A fiendish tavern on the border of the Abyss and the Hells, where fiends broker deals and sup the soul alcohol served by the night hag bartender. 
- An ancient azer holds the secret to crafting a legendary weapon at a molten forge on the Plane of Fire. 
```
