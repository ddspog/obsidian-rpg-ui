# Arctic
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
The cold regions of the world include tundra, taiga, ice, snow, glaciers, and iceberg-filled waters–and frequently get even more dangerous in winter. [[Arctic]] terrain features cold winters and cool summers, and most of its water falls in the form of snow. Arctic and subarctic life is adapted to short growing seasons, extreme cold, and long periods of sunlight and cold darkness. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in arctic terrain include: 
- Icy caverns carved into a glacier are home to a white dragon or a family of frost giants. 
- A bleak, flat land of endless snow hiding camouflaged dangers, like remorhazes or winter wolves. 
- Cold waters filled with icebergs, orcas, and the occasional [[Bear, Polar|polar bear]]. 
```
