# Grassland
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
Any land dominated by grasses and bushes, typically between forests and deserts. [[Grassland|Grasslands]] feature sparse stands of trees and large swaths of their namesake grass, often standing 3 to 10 feet tall depending on rainfall. Grassland life features an abundance of large animals, such as elephants, rhinoceroses, and lions. The open nature of grassland terrain means most creatures interact as predator, prey, symbiotic helper, herd protectors, and so forth. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in grassland terrain include: 
- A small seasonal watering hole is home to a pride of lions, a pair of rhinoceroses, a flock of axe beaks, and a young yellow dragon. They all drink in an uneasy truce during the dry season. 
- A traveling pack of gnolls and their hyena companions make a temporary home in an abandoned human fort with sunbaked wooden walls and a muddy well—bones litter the ground before the fallen gate. 
- A small gathering of trees provides shade from the midday heat to a herd of wild boars. A nesting pair of harpies roosts here each spring.  
```
