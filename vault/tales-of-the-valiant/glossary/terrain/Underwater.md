# Underwater
```rpg rule.content
id: summary
---
All marine environments, regardless of type or salinity of water, count as [[underwater]] terrain. Underwater life features differing depths, with temperatures and light levels falling as depth increases. Shallower zones support significantly more flora and fauna, which rely on light and air. Deeper zones feature creatures adapted to cold, darkness, and underwater pressure, including life near deep-sea, hydrothermal vents. Examples of creatures and adventure locations in underwater terrain include: 
- A seagrass meadow where a herd of hippocampus disturb resting crabs as they graze. 
- A kelp forest inhabited by sharks, octopi, and swarms of quippers. 
- A deep-sea trench that serves as the lair of a kraken, its territory littered with shipwrecks and whale bones. 
```
