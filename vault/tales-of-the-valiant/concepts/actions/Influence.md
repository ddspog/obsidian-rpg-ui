# Influence
```rpg feature.details
type: action
icon: "🗣️"
summary: Try to sway a creature — pick how, and make the check against its attitude.
skills: [Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Animal Handling]
---
When you take the [[Influence]] action, you try to change a creature's mind or get it to do something. Pick how you go about it:

- [[Persuasion]] — persuade a creature that understands you.
- [[Deception]] — deceive a creature that understands you.
- [[Intimidation]] — intimidate a creature.
- [[Performance]] — amuse a creature.
- [[Animal Handling]] (WIS) — coax a beast or a monstrous creature.

The rest are CHA checks. How readily the creature goes along depends on its attitude toward you: a friendly creature is willing, a hostile one resists, and an indifferent one may need the check to tip it.
```
