# Study
```rpg feature.details
type: action
icon: "📚"
summary: Recall or work something out — pick the skill that fits and make an INT check.
skills: [Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion]
---
When you take the [[Study]] action, you make an INT check to recall lore or puzzle something out. Pick the skill that fits what you're studying:

- [[Arcana]] — spells, magic items, eldritch symbols, magical traditions, the planes of existence.
- [[History]] — historic events and people, nations and cultures.
- [[Investigation]] — traps, ciphers, riddles, gadgetry.
- [[Nature]] — terrain, plants, animals, weather.
- [[Religion]] — deities, rituals, religious hierarchies, holy symbols.
```
