# Search
```rpg feature.details
type: action
icon: "🔍"
summary: Look for something — pick what you're looking for and make a WIS check.
skills: [Perception, Insight, Medicine, Survival]
---
The [[Search]] action lets you devote your attention to finding something. Depending on the nature of your search, the GM might have you make a WIS ([[Perception]]) check or an INT ([[Investigation]]) check. 

As a card, Search follows the System Reference Document 5.2's grouping — a WIS check with the skill that fits what you're looking for (working things out with [[Investigation]] belongs to [[Study]]):

- [[Perception]] — a concealed creature or object.
- [[Insight]] — a creature's state of mind.
- [[Medicine]] — a cause of death or an ailment.
- [[Survival]] — tracks, or food.
```
