# Hide
```rpg feature.details
type: action
---
When you take the [[Hide]] action, you make a DEX ([[Stealth]]) check in an attempt to hide, following the rules for hiding (see [[Hiding]]). If you succeed, you gain certain benefits, as described in the **Unseen Attackers and Targets** section in this chapter. 
```
