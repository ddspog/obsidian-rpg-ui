# Ribshatter
```rpg rule.content
name: Ribshatter
---
Make a single attack roll with your mace or morningstar against a [[humanoid]] target. This attack does normal weapon damage and the target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it is [[stunned]] until the beginning of its next turn. If the damage you dealt was greater than the creature’s maximum hit dice, it is stunned until the end of its next turn.
```
