# Blunted Bash
```rpg rule.content
name: Blunted Bash
---
Instead of dealing piercing damage, you can choose to deal bludgeoning damage by striking the target with the estoc’s unsharpened blade. This attack’s damage die is one size smaller than normal (1d4 if one-handed, 1d6 if two‑handed), and cannot benefit from the estoc’s special property.
```
