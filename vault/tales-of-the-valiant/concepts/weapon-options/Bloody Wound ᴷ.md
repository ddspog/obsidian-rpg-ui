# Bloody Wound
```rpg rule.content
name: Bloody Wound
---
When you hit a living creature with a [[scimitar]], you can choose to make a superficial but bloody wound. The attack deals no damage, but the target takes 1d6 slashing damage at the beginning of each of its turns until it makes a DC 10 Wisdom ([[Medicine]]) check as an action to stop the bleeding or until it receives magical healing. A creature can have only one bloody wound at a time.
```
