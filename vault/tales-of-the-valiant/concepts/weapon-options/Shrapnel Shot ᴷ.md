# Shrapnel Shot
```rpg rule.content
name: Shrapnel Shot
---
As an action, make a single attack with your crossbow against an unattended object. The attack deals double damage to the target. If the object is destroyed, it explodes into a cloud of shrapnel. Choose one of the following options: 
- Creatures within 5 feet of the object must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 1d6 piercing damage on a failure. 
- A creature adjacent to the object must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature is [[blinded]] until the beginning of your next turn.
```
