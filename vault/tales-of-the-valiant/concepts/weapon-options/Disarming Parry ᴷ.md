# Disarming Parry
```rpg rule.content
name: Disarming Parry
---
When a creature attacks you with a weapon, you may use your reaction to catch their weapon in your polearms's hook and disarm them. Make an attack roll with your polearms. If the result of this roll equals or exceeds their attack roll, their attack misses and they must succeed on a Strength saving throw or drop their weapon.
```
