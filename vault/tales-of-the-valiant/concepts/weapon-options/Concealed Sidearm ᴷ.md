# Concealed Sidearm
```rpg rule.content
name: Concealed Sidearm
---
Instead of making a Dexterity ([[Stealth]]) check to hide yourself, you may make a Dexterity ([[Sleight of Hand]]) check—contested by an active or passive Wisdom ([[Perception]]) check—to conceal your hand crossbow. As an action, you may make a weapon attack with a concealed crossbow against a creature that has not yet acted in combat. This attack has advantage.
```
