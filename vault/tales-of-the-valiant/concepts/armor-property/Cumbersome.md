# Cumbersome
```rpg rule.content
name: Cumbersome
---
This armor is heavy and difficult to move in unless you have the required STR score. If your STR score doesn’t equal or exceed the STR score listed in parentheses, your movement speed is reduced by 10 feet while you wear the armor, even if you’re proficient with it.
```
