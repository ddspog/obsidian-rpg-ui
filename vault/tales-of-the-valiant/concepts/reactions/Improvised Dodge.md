# Improvised Dodge
_Type: Reaction_

When a creature you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to attempt an impromptu evasive maneuver. Make a DC 15 Dexterity ([[Acrobatics]]) check. On a success, reduce the damage by 1d6 + your Dexterity modifier.

```rpg rule.content
name: Improvised Dodge
---
When a creature you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to attempt an impromptu evasive maneuver. Make a DC 15 Dexterity ([[Acrobatics]]) check. On a success, reduce the damage by 1d6 + your Dexterity modifier.

This ability can be used once per short rest. At 11th level, you can use it twice per short rest.
```
