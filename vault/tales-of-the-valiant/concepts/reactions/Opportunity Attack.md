# Opportunity Attack
```rpg feature.details
type: reaction
---
In a fight, everyone is constantly watching for a chance to strike an enemy who flees or passes by. Such a strike is called an [[opportunity attack]]. 

A creature (PC or monster) can make an opportunity attack when a hostile creature it can see moves out of its reach (see [[Reach]]). To make the opportunity attack, use your reaction to make one melee attack against the provoking creature (see **Making an Attack** in this chapter). The opportunity attack triggers as the creature leaves your reach, so you resolve your attack before it finishes its movement. 

You can avoid provoking an opportunity attack by taking the Disengage action (see **Actions in Combat**). You also don’t provoke an opportunity attack when you teleport or when movement is forced. For example, you don’t provoke an opportunity attack if an explosion hurls you out of a foe’s reach, you are shoved by a hostile creature, or if gravity causes you to fall past an enemy.
```
