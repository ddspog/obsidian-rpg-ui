# Ammunition
`````rpg rule.content
name: Ammunition
---
You can use a weapon that has the Ammunition property to make a ranged attack only if you have ammunition to fire from it (see **Adventuring Gear** in this chapter). Each time you attack with the weapon, you expend one piece of ammunition. You need a free hand to load a one-handed weapon, and drawing the ammunition from a quiver, case, or other container is part of the attack. At the end of an encounter, you can recover half your expended ammunition by taking 1 minute to search the battlefield.
`````
