## History

Bolted to the floor of the guild hall's back room since the founding
charter was signed. Anyone can draw from Ritual Supplies during a
blessed expedition; Trade Goods go only to the party treasurer.

## Notes

Members who take from Quartermaster log their claim in the wall
ledger above the chest.
